**Decided:** `plot_word_cloud_umap`, `semantic_word_cloud.png` and the `umap-learn` dependency are
removed; numba, llvmlite and pynndescent go with it. The dendrogram and distance heatmap is the one
plot.
**Why:** The UMAP plot was not useful; heat maps are what the plots build on. nixpkgs also marks
pynndescent unsupported on aarch64-linux, which blocked the arm64 OCI image.
**Cost:** None known. The Docker venv layer shrank from 516 MB to 319 MB.
